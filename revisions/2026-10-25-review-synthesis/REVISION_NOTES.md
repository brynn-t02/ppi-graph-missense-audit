## Interpretation

STRING transfer remains supported under the stated external exclusions, and original-split STRING fusion shows a small advantage over raw-embedding fusion. Homology STRING-versus-score gains and BioGRID external/incremental raw-fusion gains remain uncertain. A zero-crossing interval does not demonstrate equivalence or absence of effect. These results do not establish independent-patient/prospective validation, causal network effects or a ceiling for trained GNNs.
